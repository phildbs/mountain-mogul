🏆Most wanted

🏨Places to stay

Turn a day hill into a destination.

Hotels

Planned

Build a hotel at the base. Overnight guests stay several days, ski the first chair, and spend evenings at your bars and restaurants. Fill rooms on quiet weekdays with packages and rates.

Condos

Planned

Develop condos on land you own and sell the units for cash up front. Owners come back all season, pay dues, and expect the place to be well kept.

Ski-in, ski-out

Exploring

Lodging next to a trail is worth more. Run a trail or lift past the door and rooms fill faster.

🤝Playing together

You can already visit other resorts, compare on the leaderboards, and invite new friends with a photo of your mountain. Friend invitations now earn up to three $100,000 rewards per account. Next, more ways to play together.

Multiplayer

Exploring

Neighbouring resorts on one mountain range, competing for the same skiers, or a shared resort built with a friend.

Shared challenges

Exploring

Everyone starts the same mountain on the same day; compare profit and guest happiness at season’s end.

Interconnected passes

Exploring

Partner with another player’s resort on a joint season pass.

Visit rewards

Exploring

Earn something for visiting, liking and exploring other players’ resorts.

🏔More mountain

More mountains

Ongoing

New peaks with their own terrain, weather and crowds, beyond Juniper Ridge, Maple Notch and Raven Peak.

  • Added: Twin Notch in 2.47

A second base area

Ongoing

Open another base on a different side of the mountain, with its own access road, parking and shuttle, like Mount Snow’s.

  • Added: Twin Notch in 2.47

Terrain parks

Exploring

Jumps, rails and a halfpipe that draw a younger crowd, with upkeep to match.

Glades and backcountry gates

Ongoing

Thinned tree runs for experts, and gates to terrain that only opens on the right days.

  • Added: Glades in 2.48

Freeride bowl

Exploring

Mark out an ungroomed bowl for experts and host a freeride competition there. The bowl stays open between events.

Spring skiing

Exploring

Stay open into spring for as long as your lifts still reach skiable snow.

Summer season

Exploring

Mountain biking, an alpine slide, scenic lift rides and weddings to keep the resort earning after the snow melts.

More decorations

Exploring

More to place around your resort just for looks: trail map boards, billboards, statues, fountains, cell towers, flags and banners, snowmen and ice sculptures, fences, igloos, Adirondack chairs, and an entrance arch with your resort’s name. Maybe a small boost to how guests rate the base.

📈Running the business

Staff

Exploring

Today staff are a daily cost. Next: hire lift operators, instructors and cooks yourself, and see pay, training and morale in line speed and service.

Pumphouses

Exploring

Snowmaking that needs a pumphouse by the water: build and place them, and the snow guns they feed make better snow.

Groomer garages

Exploring

A garage for your snowcat groomers. They start the night’s grooming from there, so where you put it matters.

Marketing

Exploring

Ad campaigns, a snow report, and deals that bring in the crowds you want, when you want them.

Quick snowmaking

Exploring

One button to put snowmaking on your busiest trails.

More events

Exploring

New events beyond races, torchlight parades and pond skims, and ways to advertise après-ski.

🛠Fixes and polish

Custom mountain fixes

Planned

Designing your own mountain is paused while its terrain gets fixed, so Gentle and Easy really mean it: no surprise cliffs, and always a green way down. Custom mountains you already have still play.

Bug fixes

Ongoing

Problems players report, and crashes the game reports on its own, get fixed in small updates, most within days.

Balance

Ongoing

Prices, crowds and costs tuned so every mountain is a fair challenge, from the first season on.

Phones and tablets

Ongoing

Smoother on smaller devices, with panels and controls that fit a thumb.

Music and sound

Exploring

Calm background music and the sounds of the mountain, with a mute switch.
