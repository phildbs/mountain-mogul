Mountain Mogul privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Mountain Mogul is a free ski resort building game at phildbs.github.io/mountain-mogul. This page explains what the game stores and why.

What we store

  • Your resort. The game saves your resort (trails, lifts, buildings, money, records) in your browser and in our database so you can play on other devices.
  • Your account. If you sign in with Google or email, we receive your email address and, from Google, your name and profile picture. We use your email only to identify your account and to send sign-in links you ask for. Your name and picture are not shown to other players.
  • What you choose to share. If your resort is public, other players can see its name, the creator name you typed, its leaderboard records, and visit a copy of it. Private resorts are visible only to you.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information with advertisers.
  • We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Who handles the data

Accounts and saves are stored with Supabase. Google sign-in is provided by Google. The game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

You can make your resort private at any time from Community in the game. To delete your account and resort completely, open a request on the game's GitHub page and we'll remove them.
