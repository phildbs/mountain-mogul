Nov 16, 2026

Hello players!

One month ago, I started Mountain Mogul with a single prompt into Claude. I had no coding experience, nor did I have any technical development experience. I still don’t. Since that first prompt, there have been 1000s more: adding features, fixing bugs, making it fun! I decided to put Mountain Mogul on the web and share it not to make money, but really because I was so impressed with what AI was able to do and create.

I was, and still am, shocked at the amount of people who stumbled upon this game and started creating their own mountains and resorts. Never in my wildest dreams could I envision 100s of players logging in daily to a game I put out on the web.

I do want to thank everyone for their words of encouragement, their feature requests, and bug reports submitted through the feedback button. I also appreciate the patience of players who may have errors or instability with their resorts. I do my best to try and troubleshoot with Claude and resolve the issues as soon as I can. I do have a full time job, so I try to check for feedback during my breaks to assist with players.

As the game has gotten more features and has become more complicated since its v1 days please expect updates or at least major feature releases to slow. I’ve started to notice when adding one thing, lots of unexpected changes happen elsewhere.

Thank you all for playing!

Phil
